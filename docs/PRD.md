# A3i-Accel Frontend v2 Product Requirements

## Purpose
Explain A3i-Accel's AI offering clearly, showcase its portfolio, and turn relevant visitor interest into demo requests.

## Users
- SMB owners seeking relief from repetitive work.
- Mid-market operations leaders evaluating workflow automation.
- Investors assessing the company and product portfolio.

## Required deliverables
- Research covering five benchmark websites and three personas.
- Figma sitemap, wireframes, and high-fidelity homepage and one product page, each on desktop and mobile.
- Responsive Next.js/React and Tailwind website deployed on Netlify.
- Ask A3i concierge grounded in supplied A3i content, routing learn, pricing, and demo intent.
- Structured demo leads containing name, company, and need, submitted to an API.
- GitHub repository, README, ten-slide presentation, and live demo.

## MVP
- Homepage explaining Apps, Ants, and Agents with a concrete workflow demonstration.
- Portfolio cards and one complete product detail page using a reusable template.
- Demo request form and shared lead endpoint.
- Accessible Ask A3i widget with grounded answers and unsupported-question handling.

## Constraints
- Follow the assignment's black and dark-blue brand restriction and supplied official logo.
- Do not invent pricing, integrations, customer claims, or product capabilities.
- Resolve naming differences between assignment and live site before finalising public content.

## Success criteria
- Four of five testers explain A3i's purpose after a five-second exposure.
- Lighthouse performance and accessibility scores each reach at least 90.
- Core journeys work on mobile and with keyboard navigation.
- Concierge answers ten approved sample questions correctly and refuses off-topic requests.
- Lead submission reports success only after the backend confirms acceptance.

## Deferred scope
Every-product pages, theme switching, voice interaction, bespoke CMS, and authentication are outside the initial MVP.

## Source precedence
The assignment defines required deliverables; the research provides recommendations. Research proposals do not automatically expand scope. The user can revise project requirements.
